  zh: |
    貝殼被磨到零點幾毫米厚的時候,才開始有顏色可言。
    這個系列做的事很單純:把貝片一片一片嵌進黑漆地裡,讓它自己去接光。漆地愈黑,貝殼的虹光愈亮 —— 兩者不是對比,是互相成立的關係。
    我不在貝片上加色。它反射出什麼顏色,取決於你站在哪裡看。
  en: |
    Shell only begins to have colour once it has been ground down to a fraction of a millimetre.
    This series does something simple: set the shell into a black lacquer ground, piece by piece, and let it catch the light on its own. The blacker the ground, the brighter the iridescence — not a contrast so much as a mutual dependence.
    I add no colour to the shell. What colour you see depends on where you are standing.
