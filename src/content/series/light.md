title:
  zh: 光的容器
  en: Vessels of Light
period: 2022 –
text:
  zh: |
    漆最迷人的不是顏色,是它反光的方式 —— 不像玻璃那樣直接,而是把光含在裡面,慢慢放出來。
    這個系列把螺鈿、銀箔、研出蒔繪當成調光的工具:埋進去、再磨出來,讓光有深度,而不只是亮。
    作品完成的那一刻其實不是終點。漆會持續氧化、變透,十年後它會比現在更好看。
  en: |
    What is compelling about urushi is not its colour but the way it holds light — never as directly as glass, but taking the light in and letting it out slowly.
    Here raden, silver leaf and togidashi maki-e become instruments for tuning that light: buried, then ground back, so that brightness gains depth.
    The moment a piece is finished is not the end. Urushi keeps oxidising and clearing; in ten years it will be better than it is today.
