  en: |
    Lacquer is applied one layer at a time; each must cure and be polished flat before the next. A single vessel often takes half a year.
    This series digs back into the layers that were covered over — abrading through the surface with charcoal so the colours beneath surface on their own.
    I cannot fully control what the section will become. The parts I cannot control are usually the best ones.
