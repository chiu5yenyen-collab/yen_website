  zh: 和合
  en: Harmony

text:
  zh: |
    這個系列在問一件事:兩種不相容的材質,能不能在同一個器身上共處。
    木胎與麻布胎、生漆與礦物顏料、手作的不規則與器形的秩序 —— 它們在乾燥的過程中彼此拉扯,留下的痕跡我不修掉。
    漆需要濕度與時間才會硬化,這件事決定了創作的節奏:我無法趕它,只能等它。等待本身也成了作品的一部分。
  en: |
    This series asks one question: can two incompatible materials live together in a single body?
    Wood core and hemp cloth, raw urushi and mineral pigment, the irregularity of the hand against the order of the form — they pull at one another as they cure, and I leave the marks they make.
    Urushi hardens only with humidity and time, and that sets the rhythm of the work: I cannot hurry it, only wait. The waiting becomes part of the piece.
